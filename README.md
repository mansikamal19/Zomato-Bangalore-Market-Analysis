# Zomato Bangalore Market Analysis

### Data Analytics Portfolio Project | Python • MySQL • Power BI

## Project Overview

Analyzed **51,717 restaurant records** from Zomato's Bangalore dataset to understand restaurant distribution, customer convenience features, pricing patterns, ratings, and market opportunities.

The project combines **Python for data cleaning, MySQL for business analysis, and Power BI for interactive dashboards** to generate actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python (Pandas, Jupyter Notebook):** Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **MySQL:** Business analysis using 15 SQL queries
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development
- **Excel/CSV:** Dataset storage and validation

## Key Business Insights

- **59%** of restaurant records offer online ordering.
- **12.47%** provide table booking facilities.
- **3.70** is the average restaurant rating.
- **₹555.43** is the average dining cost.
- **54.73%** of restaurant records belong to the budget segment (below ₹500).
- **34.39%** fall in the mid-range segment (₹500–₹1,000).
- **10.21%** belong to the premium segment (above ₹1,000).

## Power BI Dashboard

Developed a three-page interactive dashboard:

1. **Market Overview** — Restaurant distribution, online ordering, table booking, and key performance indicators.
2. **Restaurant Performance** — Restaurant ratings, dining costs, and location-level comparisons.
3. **Market Opportunities** — Price segments, high-value locations, and potential business opportunities.

## Business Recommendations

- Evaluate expansion opportunities in locations with strong ratings and competitive dining costs.
- Explore online ordering opportunities for restaurants without digital ordering facilities.
- Assess customer demand and operating costs before entering premium-priced markets.
- Use location-level rating and pricing insights to support market positioning decisions.
